## Models
- **Microsoft's MAI-DxO orchestrator out-diagnoses physicians on hard cases in a Science paper** — The multi-model diagnostic orchestrator solved 83% of complex NEJM cases versus 36% for primary-care physicians working alone, per a paper published Oct 22. It is one of the first peer-reviewed results for a multi-agent orchestration setup in medicine. ([source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/microsofts-mustafa-suleyman-says-its-ai-outperforms-doctors-on-hard-cases-as-he-chases-medical-superintelligence/)) `microsoft-mai-dxo-science-paper`

## Agents and developer tools
- **Walmart opens an MCP server, Agent Gateway, for third-party shopping agents** — Announced Oct 21, it lets approved agents search inventory and buy for a shopper; per-agent "Agent Profiles" (rolling out next month) support approval of every purchase or a pre-approved spending cap, and agents never see payment data. A direct contrast to retailers blocking outside agents, and a concrete pattern for scoped agent purchasing. ([source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/21/while-amazon-blocks-ai-agents-walmart-rolls-out-the-welcome-mat/)) `walmart-agent-gateway-mcp-server`
- **FrontierCS 2 benchmarks agents on end-to-end computer-science R&D** — The benchmark scores full agent harnesses, not bare models, and ships public leaderboards. ([source](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.29932)) `frontiercs-2-benchmark-release`
- **LASAGNA study: stacking "best" RAG techniques delivers far less than predicted** — Across 450 configurations, combinations predicted +47% gains but delivered +17%, a 41% median composition penalty over 17 combinations. Useful evidence against blindly stacking retrieval tricks. ([source](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.29722)) `lasagna-rag-composition-study`

## Standards and protocols
- **Agentic AI Foundation adopts Agent Skills, Open Responses, OpenEnv and OpenSandbox** — At AGNTCon + MCPCon North America (San Jose, Oct 22–23), the Linux Foundation's AAIF took in Anthropic's Agent Skills format, OpenAI's Open Responses spec (based on the Responses API), Hugging Face's OpenEnv RL environments and Alibaba's OpenSandbox, bringing it to 10 projects. SKILL.md-style skills now sit under neutral governance next to MCP and A2A. ([source](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/agentic-ai-foundation-announces-four-new-projects-agent-skills-open-responses-openenv-and-opensandbox)) `aaif-agent-skills-open-responses-openenv-opensandbox`
