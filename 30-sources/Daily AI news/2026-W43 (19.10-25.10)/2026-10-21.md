## Safety, security and policy
- **LiteLLM 1.107.0 on PyPI was a credential stealer with a GitHub-polled backdoor** — The release, built from attacker-modified source, ran a `.pth` stealer on every Python start (SSH, cloud, CI/CD, LLM provider keys) and a `sitecustomize.py` daemon fetching commands. 1.106.2 and the 1.107.0 RCs are reported clean; PyPI removed 1.107.0. Anyone who installed it should rotate credentials. ([source](https://safedep.io/litellm-pypi-compromise-credential-stealer-backdoor/)) `litellm-1-107-0-pypi-compromise`
- **Wiz, using frontier models, finds unauthenticated PHP RCEs patched today** — CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619 (CVSS up to 9.2) affect PHP versions back to 5.4; Wiz used GPT-5.6 Sol and Gemini 4 Argon to chain and adapt exploits. PHP shipped patches and Cloudflare deployed WAF rules at disclosure. ([source](https://www.wiz.io/blog/php2shell-php-rce)) `wiz-php2shell-php-rce`
