# AI news — 2026-10-19

> Collected by the daily-ai-news routine. Draft: not verified, not in my own words.

## Models
- **Inception Labs releases Mercury 3, a diffusion LLM with 1M context** — Mercury 3 runs at about 1,068 tokens/s on NVIDIA GPUs and grows context from 260K to 1M tokens, priced at $0.20/$0.70 per million input/output tokens (half that during a launch discount). It is OpenAI-API compatible and available via Inception's API, OpenRouter, Baseten and Vercel; Inception claims it beats GPT-6 Luna and Claude Haiku 5.5 on several benchmarks but publishes no scores in the post. ([Inception Labs](https://www.inceptionlabs.ai/blog/introducing-mercury-3)) `inception-mercury-3-release`
- **MiniMax ships M3.1 Flash with 1M-token input** — A proprietary, always-thinking model taking text, image and video input, listed at $0.15 input / $0.60 output per million tokens. No benchmark scores are published yet. ([llm-stats](https://llm-stats.com/models/minimax-m3.1-flash), [MiniMax](https://minimax.io/blog/minimax-m31f)) `minimax-m3-1-flash-release`
- **Cloudflare launches Clairic, a small extraction model on Workers AI** — Cloudflare reports a Clairic-based claims pipeline ran 3.2x faster and 49% cheaper than an all-LLM baseline. It continues Cloudflare's push into small task-specific models after the Clef decision models. ([Cloudflare](https://blog.cloudflare.com/clairic-extraction-model/)) `cloudflare-clairic-extraction-model`
- **Xiaomi releases Lens-0, a 4B multimodal model for grounding** — The paper reports +5.6 points over a Qwen3.5 baseline on referring-expression splits and 68.0 gIoU on ReasonSeg, plus a new LensBench. ([Hugging Face paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.23279)) `xiaomi-lens-0-release`

## Agents and developer tools
- **AWS open-sources TorchNeuron, a native PyTorch backend for Trainium** — Trainium becomes a standard PyTorch device (`.to('neuron')`) via PrivateUse1, with eager mode, `torch.compile`, FSDP2/DTensor and NKI custom kernels; Apache 2.0, public beta from Oct 20 at PyTorch Conference. Deepgram reports about 60% more audio seconds per dollar than its GPU baseline. ([PyTorch blog](https://pytorch.org/blog/introducing-torchneuron-native-pytorch-backend-for-aws-trainium/)) `aws-torchneuron-open-source-beta`
- **Laminar releases flow-1, an open model that flags silent agent failures** — An RL-trained detector that Laminar says matches GPT-4o detection quality at 23x lower cost; Apache 2.0 and self-hostable. ([Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50154029)) `laminar-flow-1-agent-failure-detector`
- **OfficeTown benchmark: best model misses critical criteria on 1 in 5 office tasks** — Across 406 simulated workplace tasks, GPT-6 Astra leads at 74.4% and Claude Opus 5.5 scores 50.7%, but the top model still fails P0 criteria on 20.8% of tasks. ([Hugging Face paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.23194)) `officetown-benchmark-paper`
