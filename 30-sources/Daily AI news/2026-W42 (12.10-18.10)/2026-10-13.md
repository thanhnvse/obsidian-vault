## Agents and developer tools
- **Anthropic replaced git with an in-house source control system, Scion, for its monorepo** — Daily merges grew from ~2,500 in May to over 30,000 in September, mostly from always-online agents and CI jobs; Scion is server-centric (the server creates commits, diffs and merges), cut infra footprint ~200x and freed 25% CI CPU. Seven people built it plus a review tool, Antique, in five months; Anthropic says it is not a general git replacement and shares the design, not the code. ([source](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/redesigning-source-control)) `anthropic-scion-source-control`
- **DigitalOcean previewed "Agent Data" and "Agent Learning" services for self-improving agents** — A managed data layer for agent context plus tooling to observe agent behaviour, flag failures and test fixes, in public/private preview from Oct 12. Single aggregator source; not found in DigitalOcean's docs at time of writing. ([source](https://aiagentstore.ai/ai-agent-news/today)) `digitalocean-agent-data-learning-preview`
- **Apple Research described "Minions", pooling idle Apple Silicon GPUs into a distributed inference cloud** — A research paper arguing aggregated consumer Mac GPUs can be an alternative to centralised GPU clusters for ML inference. ([source](https://machinelearning.apple.com/research/distributed-gpu-cloud)) `apple-minions-distributed-gpu-research`

([source](https://www.anthropic.com/research/distillation-defenses)) `anthropic-distillation-defenses`
