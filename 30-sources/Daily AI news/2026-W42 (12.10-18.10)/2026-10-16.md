## Models
- **Reflection AI ships Beam weights under Apache 2.0** — The 501B-total / 23B-active MoE announced on Oct 5 now has public weights; Reflection reports ~80% on Terminal-Bench 2.1 at ~25k tokens per attempt versus ~80k for GLM-5.2, and Baseten serves it through OpenRouter. RL ran for four weeks on 10,500 GB300 GPUs; the benchmark claims are not independently verified. ([source](https://venturebeat.com/technology/reflections-beam-aims-to-undercut-chinese-open-models-with-fewer-tokens-not-cheaper-tokens), [source](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)) `reflection-beam-open-weights-release`
- **Microsoft AI details MAI-Base-2, a 2.8T-parameter MoE base model** — 80B active parameters, a 50T-token mixture, 16k GB200 GPUs for 38 days; Microsoft claims 4.2–12.3x better pre-training FLOPs efficiency than DeepSeek-V4-Pro-Base. It is still in post-training and will be licensed to trusted partners via Foundry, not released as open weights. ([source](https://microsoft.ai/news/frontier-pre-training-at-microsoft-ai/)) `microsoft-mai-base-2-pretraining`
- **Black Forest Labs releases FLUX 3 Image Klein (5.6B, open weights)** — Generates a 1MP image in about 4 seconds on an RTX 5090 and fits in 5.6 GB at FP8; free for commercial use under $5M revenue. ([source](https://bfl.ai/blog/flux-3-image-klein)) `bfl-flux-3-image-klein-release`
