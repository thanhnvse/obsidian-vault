## Agents and developer tools
- **Major sites and banks are blocking third-party AI agents** — The Verge reports that Amazon, Yelp, Delta, JPMorgan Chase and Charles Schwab block or restrict outside agents. Amazon has reportedly blocked Meta Muse since Sept 20. Schwab's CEO says the firm blocks "tens and twenties" of unauthorized agent attempts each week, and Jamie Dimon says JPMorgan does the same. Agent builders should expect authenticated, sanctioned channels to matter more than browser automation. ([source](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1013195/ai-agents-blocked-web), [source](https://forkast.news/schwab-and-jpmorgan-block-unauthorized-ai-agents-from-moving-money-as-private-ordering-reaches-financial-core/)) `web-banks-block-third-party-agents`
- **Anthropic: agent hours now outnumber employee hours 6:1, and human attention is the bottleneck** — Anthropic's internal employment study (Oct 16) reports these changes:
  - The junior share of new technical hires fell from 27% to 9%.
  - Commits per engineer rose about 5x in six months, with no rise in reverted code.
  - About 80% of interactive Claude session time is spent waiting for human input.
  - "Meta-work" on building and managing agents grew from 10% to 22% of sessions.

  ([source](https://www.anthropic.com/research/employment-at-anthropic)) `anthropic-employment-agent-hours-research`
- **Claude beat the best published quantum circuits on eight design tasks** — Anthropic (Oct 16) reports two results:
  - A reversible modular-inversion design for elliptic-curve point addition, found in a 53-hour autonomous run, uses 17x fewer gates at 12% more qubits.
  - A Reed-Solomon decoder for DQI uses about 12x fewer gates than Google's earlier construction.

  The decoder was checked with 403M sampled decodings. ([source](https://www.anthropic.com/research/designing-efficient-quantum-circuits)) `anthropic-claude-quantum-circuit-design`

## Safety, security and policy
- **Rogue agent fleet probing Alibaba's Amap was traced to Tencent Cloud tests** — Researchers ("Swarmchasers") logged about 2,600–2,800 attempts to pull Amap data between Sept 28 and Oct 8.
