# AI news — 2026-10-15

> Collected by the daily-ai-news routine. Draft: not verified, not in my own words.

## Models
- **Meta releases Muse Spark Open, an 816B open-weight MoE** — 42B active parameters and a 1M-token context, under the Muse Model License with limited commercial terms. It is available through several API hosts and as downloadable weights, and Meta says it runs locally on two M5 Ultra Mac Studios with MLX. Meta calls it the most capable US open-weight model so far but publishes no benchmark scores in the post. ([Meta](https://research.meta.ai/blog/muse-spark-open)) `meta-muse-spark-open-release`
- **Nvidia ships Nemotron 3.5 Super VL and Bolt open models** — Super VL is a 120B multimodal MoE (about 12B active) for document, chart and tool-using agents. Bolt 74B-A9B targets local coding and research agents in 24GB, and Bolt E4B is due October 23. All use the OpenMDW license with recipes and datasets. The claims that it beats frontier models apply only to customized versions on five narrow tasks. ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/nvidias-new-open-nemotron-3-5-super-vl-with-post-training-beats-claude-opus-5-gpt-6-1-sol-on-specialized-task-benchmarks)) `nvidia-nemotron-3-5-super-vl-bolt-release`
- **Swiss AI publishes Apertus 1.5 release notes** — 8B and 70B models under Apache 2.0, with a 262K context (4× Apertus 1.0) and text, image and experimental audio input. It also adds an optional thinking mode. ([Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/blog/swiss-ai/apertus-v15-release-notes)) `swiss-ai-apertus-1-5-release`

## Agents and developer tools
- **GitHub makes AI Scan for pull requests generally available** — It finds vulnerabilities in PR changes for languages CodeQL does not cover (PHP, Bash, Terraform, Dockerfiles, JSP, Blazor). Findings are advisory and do not need a Copilot license. A separate changelog entry moves Agentic Autofix for code scanning alerts to GA. ([GitHub changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-10-15-ai-scan-for-pull-requests-generally-available/)) `github-ai-scan-agentic-autofix-ga`
- **Accenture caps centrally funded Claude spend at $29 per employee per month** — According to a memo reported by 404 Media, some staff had been spending up to $1,000 a month. The cap is an early sign of enterprises managing agent token costs. ([404 Media](https://www.404media.co/accenture-tells-employees-they-can-now-spend-only-29-on-claude-per-month/)) `accenture-claude-spend-cap`
