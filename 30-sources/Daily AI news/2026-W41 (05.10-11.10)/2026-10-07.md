# AI news — 2026-10-07

> Collected by the daily-ai-news routine. Draft: not verified, not in my own words.

## Models
- **Anthropic released Claude Haiku 5.5, a low-cost small model with a 1M-token context** — Priced at $0.10 input / $0.50 output per million tokens for prompts up to 100K (about 75% below Haiku 4.5), with effort controls and a reported 72.4% on OSWorld 2.1 (self-reported). ([source](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-haiku-5-5), [analysis](https://llm-stats.com/blog/research/claude-haiku-5-5-launch)) `anthropic-claude-haiku-5-5-release`
- **Mistral previewed Large 4, a 1T-parameter mixture-of-experts model** — 49B active parameters; public preview on Studio and API at a promotional $0.68/$2.09 per million tokens, with open weights promised for the end of October. ([source](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4/), [pricing](https://llm-stats.com/blog/research/mistral-large-4-launch)) `mistral-large-4-preview`
- **Z.AI shipped GLM 5.3 Fast and Google listed Gemini Nano Banana 2.1** — Both appear in the LLM Gateway timeline (Oct 7 and Oct 6); no specs are given on the timeline page. ([source](https://llmgateway.io/timeline)) `zai-glm-5-3-fast-release`
- **Perplexity released pplx-embed-v2-late embedding models** — Two sizes (0.6B and 9B) with a reported 92.4% on MADQA. ([source](https://llm-stats.com/ai-news)) `perplexity-pplx-embed-v2-late-release`
