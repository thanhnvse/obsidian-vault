## Models
- **StepFun's Step 5 Preview is live on OpenRouter** — A sparse MoE reported at ~600B total / ~27B active parameters with a 1M context, available as `stepfun/step-5-preview`; open weights are reported for October 15, but no primary StepFun announcement or license was found. ([OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/stepfun/step-5-preview), [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)) `stepfun-step-5-preview-openrouter`
- **JetBrains releases Mellum 2.1, an Apache-2.0 open model for coding agents** — 12B MoE with 2.5B active parameters, same architecture as Mellum2, with the largest gains claimed on agentic coding; multi-token prediction gives ~1.6x single-request speed. Weights are on Hugging Face; GGUF builds and a vLLM MTP head are "coming soon". ([JetBrains](https://blog.jetbrains.com/ai/2026/10/mellum2-1-gets-to-work-a-fast-open-model-for-coding-agents/)) `jetbrains-mellum-2-1-release`

## Agents and developer tools
- **Google Cloud previews a persistent "universal" Gemini agent at Gemini at Work 2026** — Runs tasks over hours or days, spins up sub-agent teams, connects to Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack, MCP servers and enterprise tools, and can route between Gemini and Anthropic Claude models under admin-set limits. Private preview only; it will ship inside Gemini Enterprise at no extra charge. ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/orchestration/google-cloud-unveils-persistent-gemini-agents-for-long-running-tasks-and-they-get-their-own-gmail-calendar-and-drive-storage)) `google-cloud-universal-gemini-agent-preview`
- **Meta's MIMESIS trains user simulators to make agent training harder** — 4B and 9B simulators reproduce realistic human behaviour so agents train against tougher counterparts; the paper reports about a 5-point lift in agent scores. ([paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.09484)) `meta-mimesis-user-simulator-paper`
