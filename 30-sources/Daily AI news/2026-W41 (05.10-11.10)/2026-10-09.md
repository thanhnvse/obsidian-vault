## Safety, security and policy
- **Anthropic launched its Cyber Mission: a critical-infrastructure program and a free OSS Scanner** — The Critical Infrastructure Defense Program starts with 11 partners (incl. CrowdStrike, Dragos, Palo Alto Networks, Rockwell Automation); OSS Scanner gives enrolled open-source maintainers periodic, model-generated vulnerability reports with PoCs and suggested fixes, not human-reviewed, with an expected true-positive rate above 90%. It extends the Cyber Verification Program noted on [[2026-10-07]]. ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-cyber-mission), [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/10/09/anthropic-oss-scanner-ai-security-scans/)) `anthropic-cyber-mission-oss-scanner`

A textbook case for least-privilege agent identities. ([Zenity](https://zenity.io/press-release/zenity-labs-discloses-agentcorruption-a-chain-of-aws-agentcore-flaws)) `zenity-aws-agentcore-agentcorruption`
